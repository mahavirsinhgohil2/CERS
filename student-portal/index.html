About This Portal

The College Event Registration System replaces paper forms with a simple online process. Students can see every upcoming event on campus and register in under a minute. Event organizers can manage events and view all registrations from one secure admin dashboard.

How It Works

1

Browse Events

Open the Events page and see all upcoming college events with date and location.

2

Fill the Form

Click "Register Now" and enter your name, enrollment number, email, department and phone.

3

Get Confirmed

Submit the form and see an instant confirmation. Your registration is saved for the organizers.
